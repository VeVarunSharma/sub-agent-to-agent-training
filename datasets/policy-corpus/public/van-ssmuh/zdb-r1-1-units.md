# Dwelling units in R1-1 SSMUH

## Summary

Use the R1-1 unit count to screen whether the proposal fits the SSMUH pathway. The demo treats up to 4 dwelling units as permitted when the project also satisfies zoning, building, and site conditions.

## Key numbers

- Permitted unit count: up to 4 dwelling units
- Conditions: lot and building design must still satisfy the other cited rules
- Applies to: principal residential use under the SSMUH pathway

## How this lands in the demo

Compare the extracted unit count against 4. Trigger `gap-unit-count-over` when the proposal includes more units or when the unit schedule is unclear.
