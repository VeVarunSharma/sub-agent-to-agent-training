# Building height in R1-1

## Summary

Use the R1-1 height cap to screen massing before detailed design review. The demo treats 11.5 m as the maximum building height for SSMUH pre-review.

## Key numbers

- Maximum building height: 11.5 m
- Evidence: height dimension from elevation drawings
- Applies to: the principal building envelope

## How this lands in the demo

Compare the proposed height against 11.5 m. Trigger `gap-height-over` when the proposal exceeds the cap or when elevation evidence is missing.
