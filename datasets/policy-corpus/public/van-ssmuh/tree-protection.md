# Tree protection triggers

## Summary

Use the tree trigger to decide when the applicant must surface arborist evidence. The demo asks for a tree assessment when any tree over 30 cm DBH sits within the proposed footprint or within 2 m of it.

## Key numbers

- Tree size trigger: over 30 cm DBH
- Location trigger: inside the proposed footprint or within 2 m of it
- Evidence: tree inventory, arborist report, or site plan tree overlay

## How this lands in the demo

Compare the tree inventory and site plan against the proposed footprint. Trigger `gap-tree-assessment-missing` when a qualifying tree may be affected and no assessment is supplied.
