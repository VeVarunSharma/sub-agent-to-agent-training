# Vancouver Building By-law Part 9

## Summary

Use Part 9 as the building-code reference for small residential buildings in the demo. The zoning screen does not replace life safety, fire separation, exiting, or construction checks.

## Key numbers

- Building code scope: Part 9 small buildings
- Evidence: code analysis, drawings, and assembly notes
- Applies to: residential buildings that follow the Part 9 pathway

## How this lands in the demo
